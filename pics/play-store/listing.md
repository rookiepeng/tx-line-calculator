## Full description (4000 characters max)

Tx-Line Calculator is a transmission line calculator for RF and microwave engineers, students and hobbyists. Enter the physical dimensions of a line to get its characteristic impedance and electrical length, or enter the impedance you need and let the app find the dimensions.

SEVEN LINE TYPES
• Microstrip line
• Coupled microstrip line
• Stripline
• Coupled stripline
• Coplanar waveguide (CPW)
• Grounded coplanar waveguide (GCPW)
• Coaxial line
• Asymmetric (offset) stripline
• Coupled (edge-coupled) coplanar waveguide, with differential and common mode impedance
• Twisted pair and parallel wires, with wire gauge (AWG) presets
• Rectangular waveguide (TE10), with standard WR sizes

ANALYZE AND SYNTHESIZE
• Analyze: from width, gap, substrate height, metal thickness, dielectric constant and frequency, calculate the characteristic impedance (Z0) and the electrical length (phase) for a given physical length.
• Synthesize: from a target Z0 and electrical length, calculate the physical dimension you choose to solve for, such as trace width, gap or substrate height, plus the physical length.
• Coupled lines: work from Z0 and the coupling coefficient (k), or directly from the even and odd mode impedances (Z0e, Z0o).
• Coaxial lines: solve for the inner conductor radius, the dielectric radius or the core offset.
• Waveguides: solve for the broad wall width from a target TE10 wave impedance; see the TE10 and next-mode cutoffs.
• Loss: effective dielectric constant, conductor loss (with surface roughness) and dielectric loss, per unit length and over the line.
• More results: guided wavelength, propagation delay and skin depth.
• Frequency sweep: plot Z0, effective dielectric constant or loss against frequency, read values with a cursor, and export the data as CSV.
• Saved designs: name and keep any design, then reopen it later.
• Share a text summary of a design's inputs and results.
• Quick tools: VSWR, return loss and mismatch loss; dBm, mW and volts; and a quarter-wave transformer you can send straight to microstrip synthesis.
• Presets: common substrates (FR-4, Rogers, Isola, alumina, PTFE), conductor metals and copper weights.

INTERACTIVE DIAGRAMS
Every line type has a clear 3D cross-section diagram. Edit a field and the matching part of the line lights up on the diagram, so you always know what W, G, H, T, a, b or c refers to. Tap a label on the diagram to jump straight to its field.

PROVEN MODELS
The calculations use well-established closed-form models from the RF literature, including Hammerstad and Jensen and Kirschning and Jansen for microstrip, Cohn for coupled stripline, and Wadell for coplanar waveguide, with dispersion included where the model supports it.
