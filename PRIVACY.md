# Privacy Policy

**Tx-Line Calculator** (package `com.rookiedev.microwavetools`)

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Tx-Line Calculator Android app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

## Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, files, usage statistics or crash reports, and it contains no advertising or analytics libraries.

The app does not request internet access or any other Android permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

## Information stored on your device

To save you retyping, the app remembers the values and units you entered, the line type you last used, and whether you have opened the app before. This information:

- stays in the app's private storage on your device,
- is never sent to the developer or anyone else,
- is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.

## Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include the app's saved values in your device backup (for example, to your Google account), so they come back when you restore to a new device. This is handled by Android and your backup provider under their own privacy terms; the developer has no access to it. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.

## Contacting the developer

The **Help & feedback** item opens your own email app with the developer's address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email. If you do, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write, and uses them only to reply to you. Your message is never shared or sold.

## Children

The app is a general engineering tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Open source

Tx-Line Calculator is open source under the GNU GPL v3. You can review exactly what the app does in its source code: https://github.com/rookiepeng/tx-line-calculator

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
